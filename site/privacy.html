Privacy

Privacy notice

In short, your meter data never leaves your computer. If you contact us, we only use your details to reply.

The analyser

The analyser runs entirely in your web browser. Meter files you load are read on your own computer. They are never uploaded and we never see them. The same goes for everything you enter (opening hours, rates, notes) and everything you download (PDFs, spreadsheets, project files and customer dashboards).

The analyser stores nothing in your browser between visits. Save a project file if you want to come back to your work.

Enquiries

If you send the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, organisation, phone, sector, number of sites and message. We only use them to reply to your enquiry and any follow-up you ask for. We don't sell them or add you to a mailing list.

Kinetic Energy Consulting is responsible for the details you send (the data controller). The form is sent through Formspree, a form-handling service, which emails your enquiry to us and keeps a copy in our Formspree account on our behalf. Formspree may process data outside the UK; its own privacy policy explains how.

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up you ask for. We then delete them, from our email and from Formspree, unless we have to keep them for a legal reason. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts are served from this site rather than a third party.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing pjohnston663@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
