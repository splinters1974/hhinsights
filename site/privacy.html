Privacy

Privacy notice

In short, your meter data never leaves your computer. If you contact us, we only use your details to reply.

The analyser

The analyser runs entirely in your web browser. Meter files you load are read on your own computer. They are never uploaded and we never see them. The same goes for everything you enter (opening hours, rates, notes) and everything you download (PDFs, spreadsheets, project files and customer dashboards).

If you choose "Size solar for this site" on a site's Solar tab, the analyser opens our sister site, the UK Solar Calculator, in a new tab. It passes only that site's annual use, the estimated share of solar output used on site and, if you entered one on the Solar tab, the site postcode, in the part of the web address after "#", which browsers do not send to any server. Your readings are not passed. The calculator uses the postcode to set the sunshine for your area and to fill in its map search; it is sent to Google Maps only if you then choose to search the map there.

The analyser stores nothing in your browser between visits. Save a project file if you want to come back to your work.

If you create a customer edition

Before the analyser makes a customer edition (a locked dashboard for handing to someone else), it asks for your name, your organisation and your work email, and whether you would like to hear about licences and updates. It sends those details to us by email, with the number of sites in the edition and the date. It does not send the meter data, the site names, any figures or anything else in the file.

We ask because customer editions are how dashboards are handed to others, and using the analyser to produce work for clients needs a licence under our terms of use. We use the details to know who uses the feature and to contact you about licensing, which is in our legitimate interests. If you tick the box, we will also tell you about licences and updates, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing us.

Kinetic Energy Consulting is the data controller. The details are sent through EmailJS, an email-sending service, which passes them to our inbox. EmailJS may process data outside the UK; its own privacy policy explains how. We keep these details for 24 months from our last contact with you and then delete them, unless we have to keep them for a legal reason. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Enquiries

If you send the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, organisation, phone, sector, number of sites and message, plus the page on this site you came to the form from. We only use them to reply to your enquiry and any follow-up you ask for. We don't sell them or add you to a mailing list.

Kinetic Energy Consulting is responsible for the details you send (the data controller). The form is sent through Formspree, a form-handling service, which emails your enquiry to us and keeps a copy in our Formspree account on our behalf. Formspree may process data outside the UK; its own privacy policy explains how.

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up you ask for. We then delete them, from our email and from Formspree, unless we have to keep them for a legal reason. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Every download from the analyser (PDFs, spreadsheets, chart images and customer editions) is marked "Made with Half Hourly Insights", but that is printed in the file; it does not track you. Fonts are served from this site rather than a third party.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing pjohnston663@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
